SERVICES

Strategic consulting and engineering solutions for the energy sector.

Management Planning and assessments Electricity market Renewable energy Small municipalities and utilities

Management

Management consulting focused on organizational development, strategic planning, and efficient business operations. We support utility companies through change management, corporate governance, risk management, project development, and leadership solutions. Our services also include the implementation of customized management tools, concession support, executive selection, and communication strategies tailored to the evolving energy sector.

Planning and Assessments

We provide expert planning and technical assessment services for complex energy infrastructure projects. Our expertise includes project management for substations and control systems, energy data management, hydropower development, due diligence, investment evaluations, network assessments, and energy efficiency programs. By combining technical knowledge with economic analysis, we help utilities and industrial clients make informed, sustainable investment and operational decisions.

Electricity Market

We support clients in navigating the evolving electricity market through strategic planning, energy data management, and regulatory compliance. Our services include the development of market strategies, implementation of energy data management systems, preparation of legally compliant energy contracts, and the design of Service Level Agreements (SLAs). We help energy producers, utilities, and consumers optimize operations while ensuring efficiency, transparency, and compliance with current market regulations.

Renewable Energy

We provide engineering and consulting services for renewable energy projects, including small hydropower plants, photovoltaic systems, wind farms, and biogas facilities. From feasibility studies and system design to decentralized energy concepts and support for renewable energy incentive programs, we help clients develop sustainable, efficient, and future-oriented energy solutions.

Small Municipalities and Utilities

We support municipalities and small utility companies in managing organizational change, strategic development, and operational leadership. Our services include interim management, cooperation project coordination, contract preparation, policy development, and representation in regulatory or industry bodies. By combining economic, political, and technical expertise, we help local organizations strengthen their long-term sustainability and performance .
